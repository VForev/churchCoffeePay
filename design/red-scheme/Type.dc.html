Red is a verb. If something is red, tapping it does something. The moment red also means “sold out” or “heading”, nobody can tell what is a button any more.
Nothing is smaller than 44 px to tap, or 12 px to read. This is used one-handed, standing, in a queue, by people who left their glasses in the pew.
The label has no scheme. A thermal printer is one bit — black or nothing. Emphasis there is bold, or white out of a black chip, and it is decided in labels.ts so the preview cannot drift from the roll.
